Privacy Policy

Last updated July 1, 2026 · Operated by Stone Whisper

This Privacy Policy describes how Stone Whisper (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit or make a purchase from our store. By using the site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly and information that is gathered automatically as you browse:

  • Information you give us — your name, shipping and billing address, email address, phone number, wrist size, and any birth-date or birth-time details you choose to enter into our element-matching tool or provide with a custom order.
  • Order and payment information — items purchased and order totals. Card and payment details are entered with and processed by our payment providers; we do not store full card numbers on our own servers.
  • Information collected automatically — your device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, referring URLs, and similar usage data, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see “Cookies” below).

How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil, and ship your orders, and to size and assemble custom bracelets.
  • To run the stone-matching tool — birth details you enter are used to read your chart and suggest stones; this is a structured analysis method, not a prediction or guarantee.
  • To communicate with you about orders, respond to enquiries, and — where you have opted in — send marketing emails you can unsubscribe from at any time.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, secure the site, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To understand how the store is used so we can improve it.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep your cart working, remember preferences, measure traffic, and — where permitted — support advertising measurement. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the store work. Where required by law, we will request consent before setting non-essential cookies.

Payment processing

Payments are handled by third-party payment processors integrated with our store platform. When you pay, your payment information is transmitted directly to and processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details needed to fulfil your order.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the store — for example our ecommerce platform, payment processors, and shipping carriers — and only as needed to perform those services, or where required by law or to protect our rights.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the store, fulfil orders, and meet legal, accounting, and reporting requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of marketing. To make a request, contact us at support@stonewhisper.co. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children’s privacy

The store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above reflects the current version. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@stonewhisper.co.

Refund Policy

Last updated July 1, 2026

A bracelet has to feel right on your wrist. If yours doesn’t, here’s exactly when you can return it and how the refund works. For step-by-step return instructions, see our Returns page.

Return window

You may request a return within 30 days of delivery, provided the bracelet is unworn, in its original condition, and returned with its pouch and care card. Once your return is received and inspected, an approved refund is issued to your original payment method; please allow time for your bank or card issuer to post it.

How to start a return

  • Contact us at support@stonewhisper.co with your order number to request a return.
  • We’ll confirm whether the item qualifies and send return instructions; items sent back without prior approval may not be accepted.
  • Full return steps and any return-shipping details are on the Returns page.

Custom and made-to-order pieces

Bracelets assembled or sized to your wrist, and strands built around your chart in the Studio, are made to order. Because they are personalised, they are not eligible for standard change-of-mind refunds unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund, including any return shipping, at no cost to you.

Refund timing and method

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. Original shipping charges, where any applied, are non-refundable for standard returns and refunded when the return is caused by a damaged, defective, or incorrect item.

This Refund Policy is part of, and should be read alongside, our Terms of Service.
