The details

Every bracelet is hand-assembled and sized to your wrist after you order, so it isn’t pulled off a shelf. Assembly is included inside the overall 10–15 business-day window below — you don’t add it on top.

US orders arrive within 10–15 business days of ordering. That window covers both hand-assembly and transit. Business days don’t include weekends or holidays.

Yes. As soon as your order leaves us, we email a tracking number to the address on your order so you can follow it to your door. If it hasn’t arrived by day 15, reply to that email and we’ll look into it with you.

Your bracelet travels inside a padded stone pouch — the same pouch you’ll keep it in at home — placed in a protective outer mailer so the beads arrive unmarked. A short care card is tucked in with it.

Yes. International shipping is a flat $15. Delivery times vary by destination, and any customs duties or import taxes are the recipient’s responsibility.

Because strands are assembled to order, reach out as soon as possible if your shipping address changes. We can usually update it any time before the order ships.
