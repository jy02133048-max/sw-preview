Care Guide

Made to be lived in.
A few small habits keep it that way.

Natural stone asks for very little. A few simple habits keep the colour true, the beads clear, and the cord strong for years. Five habits, biggest first — the rest is optional.

Out of strong sun

Off before water

Wipe it clean
A soft, dry cloth after wear keeps the beads bright. No chemicals.

Do

  • Wear it often. Worn regularly, the beads pick up a soft, hand-polished sheen — stone likes being lived with.
  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth after a long day, especially in summer.
  • Take it off before bed, water, and workouts — it lasts longer when it isn’t stretched or soaked.
  • Store it flat in the pouch when you’re not wearing it, away from windowsills.
  • Put it on last — after lotion, perfume, and hairspray have dried.

Don’t

  • Leave it in strong, direct sun for hours — UV can fade coloured stones over time.
  • Shower, swim, or sleep in it. Water and constant tension wear the elastic cord.
  • Soak it or use chemical cleaners, ultrasonic machines, or alcohol.
  • Yank it on and off. Roll it gently over your hand to spare the cord.
  • Toss it loose in a bag with keys or hard jewellery that can chip a bead.

The simple routine

Five habits. None of them take a minute.

01

Keep it out of prolonged strong sun

Worn day to day, your bracelet is fine in daylight. What it doesn’t love is sitting for hours in direct, intense sun — a hot car dashboard, a south-facing windowsill.

Why: amethyst and many coloured quartzes get their colour partly from natural irradiation. Strong, sustained UV can slowly lighten that colour. Shade keeps the purple deep.

02

Wipe with a soft, dry cloth

After wear, give the beads a gentle wipe with a soft microfibre or lint-free cloth. That’s all most stones need to stay clear and bright.

Why: skin oils, lotion, and dust dull the surface over time. A quick wipe lifts them without any liquid or chemicals near the cord.

03

Take it off before showering & swimming

Remove your bracelet before showering, swimming, the gym, or washing dishes. Set it somewhere safe — ideally back in its pouch.

Why: hot water, soap, chlorine, and salt water are hard on the elastic cord and can leave residue between beads. Dry stone lasts longer.

04

Store it in the pouch

When you’re not wearing it, rest the bracelet flat inside the padded stone pouch it arrived in — not loose in a drawer or bag.

Why: stones are hard but not unbreakable. Knocking against keys, coins, or other jewellery can chip an edge. The pouch keeps each bead protected and out of the light.

05

Be kind to the elastic cord

Roll the bracelet on and off over your hand rather than stretching it wide. Put it on last, after lotion and perfume have dried.

Why: the strand is sized to your wrist on an elastic cord. Gentle handling and dry skin keep that elastic supple far longer.

A note on the cord

The cord is the one part that wears.

Your stones will outlast all of us. The elastic that holds them is the one part that ages — gradually, with stretching, water, and time. Treated gently, it serves for years.

If it ever loosens or you’d like it re-strung, reach out — we’d rather restring a bracelet you love than have you retire it.

Optional · a small ritual

If you enjoy it, set it down now and then.

This part is for those who enjoy it — nothing here is required to keep your bracelet in good shape. Some people like to set their stone down now and then, the way you’d clear a desk before starting fresh. Treat it as a quiet moment, not a rule.

  • Soft moonlight. Leave it on a sill overnight under the moon — gentle, indirect light that won’t fade the colour the way harsh sun can.
  • A breath of smoke or incense. Pass it briefly through the scent of sage, palo santo, or incense you like.
  • Sound. A bell, a singing bowl, or simply a slow minute of quiet near it.
  • A clear intention. Hold it for a moment, name one thing you want for the next few weeks, and put it back on.

We frame this honestly: it’s a personal ritual, not a claim. The care steps above are what actually protect the stone and cord. Avoid water-soaking and direct sun even when “cleansing” — they undo the care, not help it.
