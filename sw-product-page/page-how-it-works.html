Step 2, up close

Your birth moment is just data the engine can weigh.

Your chart turns a date and time into a structured map of your elements. From it, the engine reads which you carry a lot of, and which are missing. That imbalance is the whole point: the right stone leans toward the force your chart is short on — not the one it already has in excess.

It runs the same arithmetic for everyone. There’s no mood, no day-of-the-week answer — give it the same birth details and you get the same chart every time.

  • Your chartYear, month, day, and hour of birth — the four parts of one moment.
  • Element balanceHow much of each natural force your chart carries — and which one dominates.
  • Where you run thinThe element your chart is short on — the gap a matched stone is meant to fill.
  • Stones that fitA shortlist mapped to your elements — and a clear note when a stone doesn’t.

The honest part

If a stone isn’t your fit, we’ll say so.

Most shops will sell you any crystal and call it fate. We’d rather lose the sale than hand you the wrong stone. If your chart is already heavy on one force, the engine won’t pile on more of it — it points you to the stone that balances it.

“Honestly? I’m not your best fit. Your chart already runs hot on Fire — you’d feel steadier in something that cools.”
