The basics

Our 30-day return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. To be accepted, the piece needs to come back to us unworn and in its original condition — in the padded stone pouch it arrived in, with the care card.

  • Unworn, undamaged, original condition
  • Returned in the original pouch with the care card
  • Requested within 30 days of delivery

Natural stone varies bead to bead — colour and banding differ from piece to piece because it’s grown, not dyed. That natural variation isn’t a defect and isn’t grounds for a return on its own, but if something arrives damaged or wrong, we’ll make it right (see below).

Step by step

How to start a return

Email us

Write to support@stonewhisper.co within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and which piece you’re returning.

Get your instructions

We’ll reply with return instructions and the address to ship to. Please wait for this before sending anything — it helps us match your package to your order.

Pack it safely

Place the bracelet back in its stone pouch and a padded mailer. Stone is hard but beads can chip — pack it so it can’t move around.

Ship it back

Send it to the address we provide. For standard change-of-mind returns, return postage is paid by the customer. If the item arrived damaged or incorrect, we cover the return shipping.

Different size or stone

Exchanges

Need a different wrist size, or want to switch to a different stone altogether? We’re happy to exchange an unworn piece within the same 30-day window.

  • Size swap — same stone, we just restring to your size (S 15cm · M 17cm · L 19cm).
  • Stone swap — switch to a different bracelet. If there’s a price difference we’ll charge or refund the gap.
  • Not sure which stone fits you? The Studio reads your chart and suggests a match — the same engine behind every SW recommendation.

Start an exchange the same way as a return: email us, and tell us the size or stone you’d like instead. We’ll hold it for you where we can.

Good to know

What isn’t returnable

A few things can’t come back, mostly because they’re made or sized specifically for you:

  • Custom Studio strands — bracelets built to your chart in the Studio are made to order, so they are not eligible for standard change-of-mind refunds. If something arrives damaged or incorrect, we still make it right.
  • Worn or damaged pieces — anything that’s been worn beyond trying it on, or damaged after delivery.
  • Final-sale items, only when they are clearly marked as final sale at checkout.
  • Returns past 30 days — we can’t refund these, but email us and we’ll see what’s possible.

If something’s wrong

Damaged or wrong item

If your piece arrives damaged, or it isn’t what you ordered, that’s on us. Email support@stonewhisper.co within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number, and we’ll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you — the 30-day condition rules above don’t apply to our mistakes.

Still have a question?

A person answers, not a bot.

Not sure if your piece qualifies, or which size to swap to? Tell us what’s wrong and we’ll fix it — a real person, same day where we can.
