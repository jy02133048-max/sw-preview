Wrap a strip of paper or a tape measure snugly around the widest part of your wrist, then measure the length in centimetres. That number is your wrist circumference — match it to S, M, or L below. If you’re between sizes, size up; the elastic gives a little either way.
S fits a wrist of about 15 cm, M about 17 cm, and L about 19 cm. The strand sits comfortably with a touch of give. A bracelet should rest on the wrist, not grip it — if you like a looser, drapier feel, go one size up.

Because natural stone is never identical twice. Banding, veils, and depth of colour shift from bead to bead and strand to strand. We photograph honestly and don’t retouch the stone — what you receive is its own piece, not a copy of the listing image.
