Act 05 / Crystal trust

The stone has to survive the close look.

Natural crystal is not beautiful because it is flawless. Ice cracks, clouds, mineral inclusions, colour depth, and transparency are the value. The preview has to protect that feeling before it becomes an order.

Internal colour, ice cracks, clouds, and inclusions are natural traits, not flaws to erase. Size changes presence; a 10mm bead and a 6mm bead should carry different weight. Each recommended stone needs a clear role inside the bracelet, not just a nice name.
